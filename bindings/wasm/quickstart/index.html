How the teaching model works

A bolus enters the central compartment at time zero. Clearance removes drug at a rate proportional to concentration.

cp(t) = dose / volume × exp(−clearance / volume × t)

Concentration is in mg/L; time is in hours. The numerical engine is checked against this analytic reference after each run.
